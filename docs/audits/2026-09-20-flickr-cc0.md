# Flickr CC0 public image discovery — 20 September 2026

## Bounded implementation
Public image recovery now has a second source: Flickr search filtered to CC0 (license 9). Every returned image is rechecked on its own photo page: exact photo ID, safe-content flag, license 9 in the page model, matching CC0 URL in its ImageObject, matching license-page identity, and sufficient image dimensions. Missing or contradictory metadata rejects the image. The public search does not fall back to CC BY, PDM, or an unverified license.

Review mode retains its existing CC BY 2.0 behavior. Recovery retains its 90-second stage deadline, five-image pool, ten-download ceiling, subject metadata checks, original/byte deduplication, and transient photo handling. Flickr adds up to one search and five photo-page checks inside that deadline; this increases free network work relative to #225, not paid model usage. Network calls can finish after the stage deadline; no strict wall-clock bound is claimed.
