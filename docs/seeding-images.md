# Pre-seeding images/ for recurring abstract beats — owner reference

A to-do list Khalid fills over time, not code. Some topics have no
literal archive photo — "the market index", "the budget", "employment".
The photo-cooldown registry stops the same generic photo repeating, but
it can't make an abstract topic find a RELEVANT photo. The fix: one
good, openly-licensed photo per subject in `images/`. Local images are
the first rung of the ladder AND exempt from the cooldown — a seeded
photo wins every time.

## How (same as the portraits)

1. Find a relevant, openly-licensed photo (Commons, or واس imagery you
   can use). For evergreen subjects a stock-style photo of the thing is
   fine — a classroom, a trading floor, a ministry building.
2. Name it for the subject, drop it in `images/` (GitHub web upload).
3. Add its line to `images/images.txt`:
   `filename.jpg | keywords, كلمات | credit`
4. **The matcher keys on the TAGS column of images.txt, not the
   filename** — put every spelling a topic's keywords might use
   (Arabic and Latin) in the tags. The filename is for humans.

## Beats worth seeding, priority order

1. Tadawul / the market index — trading floor, Tadawul tower
2. The state budget / non-oil revenue — Ministry of Finance building
3. Schools / education / نافس / rankings — Saudi classroom
4. Employment / التوطين — workplace, jobs fair, Ministry of HR
   (excluded from news; valid for topic cards)
5. Real estate / housing / rents — Riyadh skyline, housing development
6. Inflation / prices — market, supermarket scene
7. Interest rates / SAMA — SAMA building
8. Tourism / giga-projects — usually have archive photos; low priority

Not for this list: named companies (auto-logo path), people (portrait
path), one-off topics that won't recur.
