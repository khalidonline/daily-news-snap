# Editorial image supplier requirements — daily-news-snap

Status: prepared for supplier review; NOT sent, purchased, approved by a supplier, or integrated.
Owner: assistant for technical evaluation; account owner for supplier correspondence and commercial acceptance.
Account: Snapchat executivesaudi. Saudi Arabic informational cards and connected stories triggered by current events.

## Required rights and delivery

1. Explicit editorial Snapchat/social-story rights, including rendered vertical cards and story video, with any worldwide distribution, archive retention, geography, event/sports or account-monetization restrictions stated.
2. Written permission to publish without visible photographer/agency credit or a public credits card. Internal source/license records will be retained. A standard subscription is not sufficient evidence of this exception.
3. Authorized API search, previews, licensing and high-resolution downloads for an automated workflow, with stable asset IDs, captions, dates, locations, creator information and machine-readable restrictions.
4. Explicit permission for automated relevance/quality review using third-party AI inference, including the permitted processors and retention terms. No model training, biometric identification or generative alteration of documentary imagery is intended. Confirm contractual permission rather than relying on an inference about API access.
5. Temporary source-image processing with no permanent local photo collection; retain licensed final outputs and license receipts. Confirm re-download, remote storage, subscription-expiry and withdrawal rules.
6. Distinct relevant photographs for each story card. Different crops, sizes or duplicate syndicated copies must not be represented as distinct photographs.
7. Written costs: pilot, API access, licensing/download charges, overages, minimum commitment, cancellation and any no-credit exception fee. No automatic purchase or budget increase.

## Coverage and acceptance test

Request review-only candidates for three subject types:
- A specifically named contemporary footballer (e.g. Mohamed Salah), with identity, event date and relevant usage restrictions.
- A Saudi cultural topic (e.g. bisht manufacture), with real location and context.
- A modern aircraft (e.g. F-35), with platform identity and date.

For each subject, verify at least three distinct photos at sufficient resolution (short side >=600px and long side >=1000px; preferably higher), rights for the intended use, delivery reliability and fit with actual card text. Samples do not authorize publishing or imply a fresh news trigger.

Suggested quote tiers: 90 and 180 distinct licensed photos per month for comparison only. These are not committed volumes or an approved spend.

These findings rule out treating the standard terms as a proven no-credit integration. They do not establish that a bespoke agreement is available or that no other suitable supplier exists.
