## User decision — 2026-09-20

The user now explicitly accepts visible source/photographer attribution. This supersedes the earlier no-visible-credit preference. The private research/source card must still never be published. Public image-specific credits can accompany each image; include the full required attribution, license reference and modification notice, as applicable. Attribution alone does not grant permission to use an otherwise unlicensed image.

This is a requirements update only: the runtime publication gate and rendered credit support have NOT been changed or verified by this document update.

## Required rights and delivery

1. Explicit editorial Snapchat/social-story rights, including rendered vertical cards and story video, with any worldwide distribution, archive retention, geography, event/sports or account-monetization restrictions stated.
2. Visible photographer/agency credit is accepted. Confirm the exact required wording and placement on each Snapchat card. Internal source/license records will also be retained; the private research/source card is never published. A no-credit waiver is no longer required.
3. Authorized API search, previews, licensing and high-resolution downloads for an automated workflow, with stable asset IDs, captions, dates, locations, creator information and machine-readable restrictions.
4. Explicit permission for automated relevance/quality review using third-party AI inference, including the permitted processors and retention terms. No model training, biometric identification or generative alteration of documentary imagery is intended. Confirm contractual permission rather than relying on an inference about API access.
5. Temporary source-image processing with no permanent local photo collection; retain licensed final outputs and license receipts. Confirm re-download, remote storage, subscription-expiry and withdrawal rules.
6. Distinct relevant photographs for each story card. Different crops, sizes or duplicate syndicated copies must not be represented as distinct photographs.
7. Written costs: pilot, API access, licensing/download charges, overages, minimum commitment, cancellation and any attribution-related requirements. No automatic purchase or budget increase.

## Coverage and acceptance test

Request review-only candidates for three subject types:
- A specifically named contemporary footballer (e.g. Mohamed Salah), with identity, event date and relevant usage restrictions.
- A Saudi cultural topic (e.g. bisht manufacture), with real location and context.
- A modern aircraft (e.g. F-35), with platform identity and date.

For each subject, verify at least three distinct photos at sufficient resolution (short side >=600px and long side >=1000px; preferably higher), rights for the intended use, delivery reliability and fit with actual card text. Samples do not authorize publishing or imply a fresh news trigger.

Suggested quote tiers: 90 and 180 distinct licensed photos per month for comparison only. These are not committed volumes or an approved spend.

## Additional source research — 2026-09-20

- Flickr: official Creative Commons catalogue includes CC BY 2.0 and 4.0 as well as BY-SA. Select and verify individual image licenses; do not treat all Flickr images as reusable. https://www.flickr.com/creativecommons/
- Wikimedia Commons: individual asset author/license/derivative requirements apply. Revisit BY assets previously excluded by the no-visible-credit gate; BY-SA also requires compatible adaptation licensing, not just a source label. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia
- ESO: default public images are CC BY 4.0 unless stated otherwise. Full unaltered readable credits required; identifiable-person commercial-use restriction and logo exclusions apply. https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/
- ESA: informational/editorial social-media use permitted under stated terms with credits for ESA-owned imagery. Third-party material may need separate permission; other commercial use needs written authorization. Some assets use CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO. https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Terms_and_conditions_of_use_of_images_and_videos_available_on_the_esa_website
- NASA: investigate asset-specific ownership/credit notices, including third-party exceptions. https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
